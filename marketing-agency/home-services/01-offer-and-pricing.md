# Home Services Marketing Agency — Productized Offer & Pricing
### Vertical: HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical

> **Positioning in one line:** "We get HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies more booked
> jobs — guaranteed — by capturing the calls you're already missing and reactivating the
> customers you already have."

You are **not** selling "marketing." You are selling **booked jobs**. Every package, every
sales conversation, and every report is framed in the contractor's own language: *leads,
booked jobs, cost-per-job, and revenue.*

---

## 1. Why this offer works for trades

Trades owners are pragmatic and ROI-driven. They don't care about "brand" or "impressions."
They care about the phone ringing and the calendar filling. Three structural facts make this
business model print money in this vertical:

1. **High job values.** One AC install ($8k–15k) or panel upgrade ($2k–4k) pays for *months*
   of your retainer. The ROI math is trivially obvious — use it relentlessly in your pitch.
2. **They're already losing money to missed calls.** The average home-services business misses
   **20–30% of inbound calls** (after hours, on a job, on another line). Every missed call is a
   $300–15,000 job going to a competitor. This is the easiest "found money" you can sell.
3. **They have a goldmine in their CRM.** A 10-year-old HVAC company has thousands of past
   customers who need annual tune-ups and aging equipment. Reactivating that list produces
   booked jobs in *days* — your fastest path to a happy client.

## 2. The productized service ladder

Three tiers. Same fulfillment playbook every time (that's what makes it automatable). Each tier
is a superset of the one below it.

### 🥉 Tier 1 — "Found Money" (Foundation)
**$1,500/mo + $750 one-time setup**

The foot-in-the-door offer with the fastest, most visible ROI. Low fulfillment cost, near-pure
automation. This is what you lead with in cold outreach.

- **Missed-Call Text-Back** — any missed/abandoned call instantly gets an automated text
  ("Sorry we missed you — what can we help with?"), recovering jobs that would've gone to a
  competitor.
- **Database Reactivation Campaign** — import their past-customer list, run a seasonal offer
  (tune-up, safety inspection) via SMS + email. *This alone typically books jobs in week one.*
- **Review Engine** — automated review requests after every completed job, routing happy
  customers to Google and unhappy ones to a private feedback form.
- **Google Business Profile optimization** — fully optimized profile, weekly posts, Q&A.
- **Monthly report** — calls recovered, reviews gained, jobs booked from reactivation.

> **Pitch math:** "If this recovers just *one* extra install a month, you're up ~$8k on a
> $1,500 spend. Everything past that is profit."

### 🥈 Tier 2 — "Always-On Jobs" (Growth)  ⭐ most popular
**$3,000/mo + $1,000 setup + ad spend (paid directly to Google by client)**

Everything in Tier 1, plus active lead generation.

- **Google Local Services Ads (LSA)** management — the #1 channel for trades. Pay-per-lead,
  "Google Guaranteed" badge, top-of-page placement. You manage bids, disputes, and lead quality.
- **Google Search Ads** management — capture "emergency AC repair near me" intent.
- **AI Receptionist / 24-7 lead capture** — AI voice agent answers after-hours calls, qualifies,
  and books straight into their scheduling software. Never miss an emergency call again.
- **Speed-to-lead automation** — every new web/ad lead gets an instant call + text within
  60 seconds (the single biggest lever on conversion rate).
- **Lead nurture sequences** — unbooked leads get followed up automatically until they book or die.
- **Bi-weekly reporting + monthly strategy call.**

### 🥇 Tier 3 — "Market Domination"
**$5,500/mo + $1,500 setup + ad spend**

Everything in Tier 2, plus multi-channel scale and dedicated strategy.

- Meta/Facebook + retargeting campaigns (great for installs, financing offers, seasonal pushes).
- Conversion-optimized landing pages per service (AC, heating, water heater, panel upgrade).
- Full local SEO program (service-area pages, citations, backlinks) to rank organically.
- Dedicated account strategist + weekly calls.
- Competitor monitoring and quarterly growth planning.

## 3. Pricing structure & psychology

| Lever | Recommendation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Setup fee** | $750–1,500 | Covers onboarding labor; filters tire-kickers; recovers cost if they churn early. |
| **Contract** | 3-month initial term, then month-to-month | Long enough to prove ROI (reactivation + reviews compound), low enough to lower the buying barrier. |
| **Ad spend** | Always separate, paid by client directly to Google/Meta | Never front ad spend. Keeps your margin clean and your books simple. |
| **Billing** | Auto-charge card/ACH on the 1st via Stripe | No chasing invoices. Automation-friendly. |

### Optional: the performance hybrid (advanced)
Once you trust your delivery, a **base + per-booked-job** model is irresistible to trades owners
because it aligns incentives. Example: **$1,000/mo base + $50 per qualified lead** (capped). Only
introduce this after you have call-tracking + attribution dialed in, or you'll leave money on the
table or argue over what counts as a "lead."

### The ROI framing script
> "A new system install is worth what to you — around $9,000? Our Growth plan is $3,000/month.
> So if we book you *one extra install per quarter* that you wouldn't have gotten, we've paid for
> ourselves three times over. And reactivation alone usually books more than that in the first month."

## 4. Guarantee (a closing accelerator)

Trades owners have been burned by agencies. A guarantee removes the risk and crushes objections.
Pick one you can actually back with the reactivation + missed-call engine:

- **"X booked jobs in 90 days or you don't pay month 4."** (Strong, but only promise a number you
  can hit with reactivation alone — that's your safety net.)
- **"We'll recover more in missed-call revenue than our fee, or it's free."** (Easy to defend.)

Never guarantee something you don't control (e.g., revenue). Guarantee *activities and recovered
leads* you can produce mechanically.

## 5. What makes this productized (and therefore scalable)

- **One onboarding checklist** regardless of client.
- **One tech stack** (see the automation-workflow doc) deployed via a cloneable template/snapshot.
- **One reporting dashboard** template.
- **Standard service-level menu** — you never do "custom" work; clients pick a tier.

This is the whole point: when fulfillment is identical for every client, one person (plus
automations) can service 10–20 accounts. That's a $30k–100k/mo business with a tiny team.
