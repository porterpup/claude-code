# Home Services Marketing Agency — Playbook
### Vertical focus: HVAC · Plumbing · Electrical

A productized, highly-automated marketing agency that sells **booked jobs** to home-services
contractors and acquires its own clients through an automated outreach engine.

## The thesis in three sentences

1. Trades owners buy **booked jobs**, not "marketing" — so everything is framed in leads, jobs,
   and cost-per-job.
2. The fastest ROI (and your safest guarantee) comes from **missed-call recovery + reactivating
   their existing customer database** — money that's already on the table.
3. Productizing delivery into one cloneable GoHighLevel template means one operator + automations
   can run 15–20 clients, and an automated audit-first outreach engine fills the pipeline at low CAC.
